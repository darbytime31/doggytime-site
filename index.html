The odor eliminator

Safe for the ones actually using the yard.

Hauling the waste off fixes what you can see. The smell is a separate problem — ammonia and bacteria soaked into the grass and soil where the piles were sitting. We treat those spots on every visit.

Included with every plan. Never an upcharge.

Safe for pets and kids once dry

It goes down as a light mist on the affected areas and dries in about 15 minutes. After that the yard is theirs again — no waiting around for the rest of the day.

Won't burn your grass

It breaks odor down biologically instead of bleaching it away, so it's safe on turf, flower beds, and anything else growing near the spots we treat.

Neutralizes, doesn't mask

No heavy perfume sitting on top of the smell. It targets the bacteria producing the odor, so the yard goes back to smelling like a yard.

Applied where it's needed

Not blanketed across the whole lawn. We treat the areas your dog actually uses, which is where the odor builds up in the first place.

Dog with allergies, sensitive skin, or a health condition? Mention it when you call and we'll walk you through exactly what we use — or skip the treatment entirely and keep the rest of your plan the same.
